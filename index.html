about me

My research interests focus on Efficient ML, World Action Models, Generative Models, and Vision Transformers.

I am particularly interested in designing novel and efficient model architectures, accelerating inference, and improving memory efficiency through architectural innovations and computational optimizations.

Research Interests

  • World Models & RoboticsWorld Models, World Action Models (WAMs), action-conditioned generation, and embodied intelligence.
  • Generative ModelsDiffusion Models and Autoregressive Video Generation.
  • Efficient AI & Model ArchitecturesLoop Transformers, Token Compression, DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA), Test-Time Training (TTT), Linear Attention variants, and Mamba / State Space Models (SSMs).
  • Computer VisionVision Transformers, Object Detection (DINO), Object Segmentation (SAM).
